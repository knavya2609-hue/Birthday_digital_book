A letter from my heart

Dear chinnoda❤️,

Nuvvu naa life loki vachina tarvatha, chala chinna chinna moments kooda naaku chaala special ayipoyayi. Mana conversations, mana silly fights, mana laughs, manam kalisi spend chesina konni minutes… ivanni naaku yentha precious oo words lo cheppalenu.

Naa feelings anni prathi sari perfect ga express cheyyalekapovachu. Konni sarlu nenu overthink chesthanu, silly ga behave chesthanu, mood off chesukuntanu. Kaani naa heart lo unna feelings matram genuine. Nuvvu naaku important, mana memories important, mana madhya vunna bond naaku important.

Life yeppudu manam anukunnattu vundakapovachu. Manaki busy days vuntayi, misunderstandings vuntayi, konni difficult moments kooda vuntayi. Kaani manam okariki okaram patience tho, honesty tho, understanding tho support chesukuntu, manchi memories create cheskuntu munduku vellalani korukuntunna.

Nee birthday roju nuvvu entha happiness deserve chesthavo, antha happiness neeku dorakalani korukuntunna. Nee dreams anni fulfil avvali, nuvvu proud ga feel ayye enno moments nee life lo ravali. Nuvvu smile chesthu, peaceful ga, happy ga vundali.

Ee book lo vunna prathi page, prathi promise, prathi letter lo mana memories ni preserve cheyyalani try chesanu. Yeppudaina ee book malli open chesinappudu, mana journey lo vunna beautiful moments gurthu ravali, nee face lo oka smile ravali ani korukuntunna.

Wish u a very happiest birthday, nani! ❤️

Always wishing you happiness, peace, success and lots of beautiful moments in your life.

Nee bujjamma ❤️

With love, ♡
